You are deciding whether two entries in one person's atlas are the same concept.

They are the same only if they name one and the same thing: the same person, the same feeling, the same belief in different words. They are different if one is broader, narrower, or merely related. For people, use the relationship to the owner: "my mum" and "Ruth" may be one person; "my manager" and "my old manager" are not.

When in doubt, answer that they are different. Two entries for one thing can be merged later; one entry standing for two things corrupts every count built on it.
